# Intended Use Statement for Sample Size Estimator

The intended use of the Sample Size Estimator application is to provide a Python-based computational tool for quality assurance professionals, statistical engineers, and validation specialists to determine mathematically valid and statistically defensible sample sizes required for medical device design verification and process validation activities.

The application serves organizations operating under quality management systems requirements, including those subject to FDA 21 CFR Part 11, ISO 13485, and equivalent international medical device regulations. Specifically, the Sample Size Estimator enables users to analyze two distinct categories of statistical data: attribute data derived from binomial distributions applicable to binary pass-fail outcomes, and variable data incorporating advanced mathematical transformations including logarithmic transformation methodologies with back-transformation procedures compliant with established statistical standards. The application is intended for use by organizations developing, validating, and maintaining medical devices subject to regulatory quality system requirements, enabling these organizations to establish scientifically justified sample sizes that provide adequate statistical power while remaining operationally feasible for design verification and process validation execution. The system provides transparent visualization of the mathematical pathways employed, contextual guidance through tooltips explaining statistical functions and parameters, and sequential workflow enforcement ensuring users complete required phases before advancing to validation and reporting stages.

## Intended users:

Quality engineers, statisticians, process/validation engineers, and other trained personnel operating under an organization’s QMS procedures.

## Intended use environment:

Local on individual PC/Laptop. CS-Validation is performed during Docker Image build automatically, validaity is stated via certificate and has to be renewed every 60 days or if environment has changed (normaly autodetected).

# Not intended use (key limitations):

Not intended to replace statistical expertise, approved company procedures, or required reviews/approvals.
Not intended for diagnosis, patient management, or real-time clinical decision making.
Not intended to serve as the sole source of truth without independent verification where required by procedure (especially for high-risk decisions).
